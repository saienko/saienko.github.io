Two workbenches

Two games, two very different toolkits.

One is about generating enough sunlight to survive. The other is about surviving what someone else built to keep you out. Both are made the same way — piece by piece, and tested until they feel right on a phone.

In development

Solar Island Defence

Tower defense on a floating solarpunk island.

A floating-island tower defense game built with a toy-box, tilt-shift art style — like looking down at a miniature diorama rather than a battlefield. You place and upgrade solar-powered turrets and self-propelled artillery to defend the island, moving between direct combat, strategic placement, and light city-building as each run grows.

  • Five-tier turret upgrade paths with clear, color-coded progression
  • Self-propelled artillery units alongside static defenses
  • A toy-box, tilt-shift art style designed for mobile readability
  • Cosmetic-only store — no pay-to-win, progress is never gated
GenreTower defense
StyleToy-box 3D, solarpunk
PlatformiOS & Android (planned)
MonetizationCosmetics only

Labyrinth of Traps (working title)

An ancient labyrinth, built trap by trap.

A game growing out of real research into ancient traps, locks, and labyrinths — starting with Egyptian-themed chambers. Rather than a generated maze, each room is designed around a specific historical mechanism, so the challenge comes from understanding how a trap actually works, not just memorizing a path.

  • Levels grounded in research into real historical trap and lock mechanisms
  • Egyptian-themed chambers as the first setting
  • Environments built and textured piece by piece, from stone walls to workbenches
  • Hand-designed rooms, not procedurally generated mazes
GenrePuzzle / exploration
SettingAncient Egypt (first chapter)
PlatformMobile (planned)
StatusDesign & research
