Privacy Policy

As an independent mobile app developer, I am committed to protecting your privacy and being transparent about how your information is handled. I don't sell, rent, or share your personal data with third parties for commercial purposes. My business model relies solely on optional premium features, ensuring my interests align with yours as a user. This policy explains in clear terms how I collect, use, and protect your information, including any personally identifiable information (PII) — data that could be used to identify, contact, or locate you individually.

Data collection practices

Account data

If you use my app without creating an account, no personal information is required — your data stays locally on your device. For users who choose to create an account for cloud sync or access to database and backup features, I must use:

  • Email address for authentication
  • Encrypted password
  • Optional name fields (if provided)

Usage analytics

To improve the app experience, I gather anonymized usage statistics through analytics services including Google Analytics for web interactions and mobile analytics platforms. These tools help me understand which features are most valuable without tracking individual users personally.

Cookie and tracking policy

Technologies used

Local storage: data cached on your device for:

  • Offline functionality
  • Improved app performance
  • User preference storage

Development tools: code libraries that help me:

  • Monitor app stability and crashes
  • Understand feature usage patterns
  • Optimize performance

Your control options

You can manage these technologies through:

  • Google Analytics opt-out browser extension
  • Device-level privacy settings for mobile apps
  • Do Not Track browser signals (where supported)

Information usage

Service provision

The limited data I collect enables me to:

  • Authenticate your account securely
  • Sync your data across devices
  • Provide customer support when needed
  • Maintain app functionality and reliability

Product enhancement

Analytics data helps me:

  • Identify popular features for continued development
  • Prioritize bug fixes and improvements
  • Make informed decisions about new features
  • Ensure app stability and performance

Security and safety

Your information helps me:

  • Prevent unauthorized account access
  • Detect and prevent fraudulent activity
  • Maintain secure payment processing
  • Protect against abuse and violations

Data sharing practices

Legal compliance

While I've never been required to do so, I may disclose user information when legally obligated to:

  • Comply with valid legal processes or government requests
  • Defend against legal claims or assert legal rights
  • Investigate illegal activities, fraud, or security threats
  • Protect user safety in emergency situations

I will notify affected users of legal data requests unless prohibited by law or court order.

Business transitions

In the event of a merger, acquisition, or asset sale, I will:

  • Maintain confidentiality protections for personal data
  • Provide advance notice to users before any data transfer
  • Ensure continued privacy protection under new ownership

User rights and data control

Accessing your data

You have the right to:

  • Review what information I have about you
  • Request copies of your stored data
  • Understand how your data is being used

Data usage objections

You may:

  • Adjust privacy settings within the app
  • Opt out of certain data collection practices
  • Contact me with specific data usage concerns
  • Request restrictions on data processing (where applicable by law)

Data storage and retention

I retain different types of information for varying periods:

  • Account information: kept for the duration of your account to maintain functionality
  • Usage data: retained until you delete your account or request removal
  • Analytics data: stored in anonymized form for product improvement purposes
  • Support communications: kept as long as necessary for customer service

All retention practices balance service provision needs with privacy protection and legal requirements.

Child protection

Children under 13 years old (or the applicable minimum age in their jurisdiction) cannot create accounts without verified parental consent as required by law. If I discover that a child's personal information was collected without proper consent, I will promptly delete that information. Parents who believe their child has provided personal information may request deletion through the app or by contacting me directly.

Security measures

I implement multiple layers of security protection including:

  • Encrypted data transmission (SSL/TLS)
  • Secure password storage with encryption
  • Regular automated backups with redundancy
  • Administrative and physical access controls
  • Industry-standard security practices

While no digital system is completely immune to security risks, I continuously work to protect your data using current best practices and technologies.

Policy updates

This privacy policy may be updated periodically to reflect changes in my practices or legal requirements. Significant changes will be communicated via:

  • Email notifications to registered users
  • In-app announcements
  • Website posting with effective date

Continued use of my services after policy updates constitutes acceptance of the revised terms.

GDPR compliance note

For users in applicable jurisdictions, I collect minimal personal information (primarily email addresses for account holders). Your data is:

  • Stored securely on cloud infrastructure
  • Accessible only to me for technical support purposes
  • Encrypted when stored and transmitted
  • Never sold, rented, or shared commercially
  • Permanently deletable through in-app controls

Contact information

I'm an independent developer passionate about creating useful mobile applications while respecting user privacy.

For questions, concerns, or requests regarding this privacy policy or your data, please reach out through my support channels.
