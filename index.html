Process

How each game gets made

No studio, no pipeline handoffs — every system, sprite, and sound gets touched by the same pair of hands, in roughly this order.

01

Design on paper

Loops, upgrade paths, and monetization get worked out in a full design document before a single asset exists — so the mechanics hold up before the art does.

02

Build the diorama

Art and systems are built as reusable, modular pieces — a turret tier, a trap mechanism, a texture set — so the whole world stays visually consistent as it grows.

03

Polish and ship

Mobile-first performance passes, playtesting, and readability checks, then a release to iOS and Android.
